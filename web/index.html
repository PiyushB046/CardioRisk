How it works

Three quiet steps.
One honest number.

From a clinical form to an explained probability in under a second.

01

Enter clinical data

Age, chest-pain type, ECG, exercise test and fluoroscopy results. Unknown fields are allowed; the model was trained with missing data.

02

The model scores it

A tuned RBF-SVM, chosen from seven candidates by repeated 5×5 cross-validation, returns a probability of heart disease.

03

Understand why

XGBoost SHAP contributions show which factors pushed the risk up or down for this specific patient.

Model comparison

Honest scores.
No hidden leakage.

Every candidate is scored on identical cross-validation folds of the training set. The test set stays untouched until the final check.

Trust & method

Quiet in the UI.
Rigorous underneath.

Locked test set

A stratified 20% hold-out that is never used for tuning or model selection.

Calibrated

A Brier score of 0.105. A predicted 70% means about 70% of those patients really have the disease.

Explainable

Per-feature contributions for every single prediction, not just global importance.

Full audit trail

Inputs, probability and model version for every prediction, stored in PostgreSQL.
